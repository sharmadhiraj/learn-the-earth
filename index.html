What am I looking at?

  • Each step, the network sees a few hundred random places on Earth, each labelled land or water, and nudges its weights to guess better.
  • Fourier bands turn the two coordinates into many waves of different sizes. Set it to 0 and the network can only draw blurry blobs. Raise it and coastlines appear.
  • Accuracy and loss are measured on 5000 places the network never trains on.
